Empires in the Dust

Lost cities, ancient secrets, and the open questions of history.

Empires in the Dust is an independent history channel. We publish illustrated short documentaries every day and longer documentaries twice a week, about vanished civilizations, strange events and the mysteries that historians and archaeologists are still debating.

We stick to the evidence: when historians disagree or the sources are thin, we say so.

Empires Autopilot

"Empires Autopilot" is the private tool we use to publish our own videos to our own channel through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public and only ever accesses the Empires in the Dust channel. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
